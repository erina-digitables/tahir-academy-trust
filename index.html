About us

Intellectual growth and spiritual development, hand in hand.

Every student is equipped with the knowledge and character to navigate the modern world while remaining rooted in their faith — taught in small weekend classes by teachers who know them by name.

Our courses

What we teach

Four routes, running every Saturday and Sunday across our branches — see Our Locations for times.

Separate classes for boys and girls — so every child can learn in a comfortable, focused setting.

Enrolment

Joining is straightforward

Come and see a class before committing. Visit any weekend during open house, or call us directly and we will help you find the right fit.

    Visit an open house

    Every weekend, 9:30am–12:30pm at Waverley Primary School, Enfield EN3 7DL. Meet the teachers and see a class. Nothing to book. Nearer Hertford? Call us to arrange a visit to Pioneer Hall.

  2. 2

    Choose the right course

    We will talk through Maktab, Hifz ul Qur'an, Sanatayn and Aalimiyyah with you and your child, and recommend the route that fits where they are now.

  3. 3

    Start with your class

    Complete a short registration and your child begins. Get in touch for current intake dates and fees.

Building future generations

Alhamdulillah, by the Grace of Allah we have set ourselves on a journey to build the future generations. Our Maktab welcomes children from age five, alongside a dedicated programme to memorise the Holy Qur'an with tajweed and our two-year Sanatayn and six-year Aalimiyyah courses — all open to both boys and girls.
